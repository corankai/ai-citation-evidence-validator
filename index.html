What the validator checks

Traceability

Stable prompt IDs, benchmark versions, answer systems, timestamps, and evidence artifacts.

Citation consistency

Whether a claimed target-domain citation agrees with the list of cited domains.

Interpretability

Separate errors and warnings, with every finding attached to a record and field.

Reproducibility

Duplicate run keys and incomplete claims that would make a later audit difficult.
